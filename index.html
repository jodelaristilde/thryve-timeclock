Connect to your Jotform time clock

Paste your Jotform API key once. It's saved only in this browser on this computer. To get it: in Jotform, click your profile picture → Settings → API → Create New Key, and set it to Full Access so this page can add and remove employee names.

Register an employee

Only employees switched to Working appear in the Your Name list on the clock-in form, so only they can clock in. Switch someone on when you ask them to work, and off when their shift is over.

Schedule a shift

Employees are paid only for time inside their scheduled shift. Clocking in early, late, or on a day with no shift is flagged on the weekly report.
